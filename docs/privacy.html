Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Better YouTube is an open-source app that runs entirely on your devices. It has no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Its developer never receives your data. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes.

Google account and YouTube data

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for the youtube.force-ssl permission through Google's own sign-in screen. It uses that access only to show and act on your YouTube account at your request: your subscriptions, playlists and liked videos, liking videos, posting comments, subscribing to channels and editing your playlists.

Requests go directly from your device to Google's servers. The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored in your device's keychain and are never sent anywhere else. YouTube data is kept on your device only as long as needed to display it.

Better YouTube's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; YouTube's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional youtube.com session

If you choose to sign in to youtube.com inside the app, for your home feed and Watch Later, that session lives in a separate web data store on your device. Its cookies are not read by the app, logged or sent to any other service.

Data stored on your devices

Your watch history, favorites, playback positions, recent searches, downloads and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is turned on, the library and playback positions are synchronized through your own private iCloud database, which Apple stores under Apple's Privacy Policy and which the developer cannot access.

Optional download server

If you configure a download server of your own, the app sends it the addresses of the videos you choose to download. That server is run by you, not by Better YouTube.

Removing your data

Signing out removes the Google tokens from your device. You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account. Deleting the app removes the data it stored on your device; data synchronized to iCloud can be deleted from your iCloud settings.

Children

Better YouTube does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project's repository, where you can also ask questions by opening an issue.
