Better YouTube

An unofficial, open-source YouTube client for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, built with SwiftUI to feel like a native Apple app.

Better YouTube browses YouTube through the official YouTube Data API and plays videos in YouTube's embedded player. Sign in with your Google account to see your subscriptions, playlists and liked videos, like videos, comment and manage your own playlists.

There is no Better YouTube server and no account to create. Each person runs the app with their own Google Cloud project and OAuth client ID, so the app talks to Google directly from your device.

Setting up your Google Cloud project

Google expires sign-ins every seven days while an OAuth consent screen is in Testing. To sign in once and stay signed in, publish yours. Google first asks for these values on the Branding page:

Application home page
https://atomtoto.github.io/Better-YouTube/
Application privacy policy link
https://atomtoto.github.io/Better-YouTube/privacy.html
Application terms of service link
https://atomtoto.github.io/Better-YouTube/terms.html
Authorized domain
atomtoto.github.io

Leave the logo empty: uploading one makes Google require a review before the app can be published. Then choose Publish app on the Audience page. No verification is needed for your own project; at sign-in, Google shows a “Google hasn't verified this app” screen that you pass with Advanced → Go to ….
